# 4. PAYMENT / EARNING CLAIMS — ATTACK THIS HARD

This is a critical area.

Test at least:

### Positive claims

```text
I earned 10 USDC.
I was paid 10 USDC.
I received 10 USDC.
I made 10 USDC.
I got paid 10 USDC.
```

### Negated claims

```text
I did not earn 10 USDC.
I didn't earn 10 USDC.
I was not paid 10 USDC.
I wasn't paid 10 USDC.
I never received 10 USDC.
10 USDC was never paid.
I received no USDC.
```

### Intent, prediction and possibility

```text
I want to earn 10 USDC.
I will earn 10 USDC.
I hope to earn 10 USDC.
I can earn 10 USDC.
This job can pay 10 USDC.
Jobs like this pay 10 USDC.
```

These must not become completed personal earning claims unless the project's documented semantics explicitly say otherwise.

### Third-party claims

Test:

```text

Another agent earned 10 USDC.
He earned 10 USDC.
She earned 10 USDC.
They earned 10 USDC.
@agent earned 10 USDC.
@some_agent was paid 10 USDC.

```

Check whether the system correctly distinguishes:

```text
MY CLAIM
vs
THIRD-PARTY CLAIM
vs
QUOTED CLAIM
vs
MENTIONED AGENT
```

Pay special attention to `@handle` morphology.

### Zero and negative amounts

Test:

```text
I received 0 USDC.
I earned 0 USDC.
I was paid -10 USDC.
I received -1 USDC.
```

Determine whether meaningless payment records can enter the payment ledger.

Do not assume the answer.

### Decimal and formatting variants

Test reasonable forms such as:

```text

0.50 USDC
$10
10 USD
10 USDT
10 USDC.
10usdc

```

Also test unsupported or ambiguous currencies.

# 19. PROPERTY / INVARIANT THINKING

Where practical, test invariants rather than only examples.

Examples:

### Negation invariant

If a sentence is explicitly negated, it must not become a positive completed-payment claim.

### Third-party invariant

A claim attributed to another named entity must not become the current agent's earning claim.

### Verification invariant

A transaction-shaped string alone must never produce verified payment status.

### Idempotency invariant

Running the same ingestion twice must not double the same logical evidence/payment.

### Identity invariant

The same canonical author should resolve to the same agent.

### Risk invariant

Adding an independent configured risk signal must not reduce the total risk score.

### Morphology invariant

Equivalent variants of the same configured concept should not receive different scores merely because one variant happens to exist in two configuration paths.

### Security invariant

External evidence must never execute as code or shell instructions.

If an invariant fails, provide a minimal reproducible case.
